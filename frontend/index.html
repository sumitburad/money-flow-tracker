Money-flow windows and rotation

The rule: price up on rising volume = inflow (money appears to be coming in); price down on rising volume = outflow. A move on thinning volume is marked “fading”. Each item is checked for the latest day, the week (5 sessions against the 5 before) and the month (20 against the 20 before), then rolled up by asset class and sector to show where money is rotating. Volume is a trading-activity proxy, not a measured fund flow. Only categories are shown, because the free price source cannot be republished.
